$\quad$ The justificatory and reliability challenges correspond to two aspects of knowledge about abstract entities, as generalized from the [[The Benacerraf problem|Benacerraf problem]] for [[Mathematical realism|mathematical realism]]. In this case, **knowledge** is “justified belief which is non-coincidentally true” ([[MATH-GU4200]] Week 7). Thus, the question of how we are able to have knowledge about abstract entities factors into the following:

- **Justificatory challenge:** Why is it rational or reasonable to believe the true statements we make about abstract entities?
- **Reliability challenge:** How do we explain the non-coincidental truth of our beliefs? “What is the correlation between what we do to arrive at the facts, and the facts?”

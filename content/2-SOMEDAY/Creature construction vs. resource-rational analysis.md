$\quad$ [[@2025icardResource]] extends [[(Resource-)rational analysis|resource-rational analysis]] to the following assumption in Grice’s [[Creature construction approach to philosophical psychology, after Grice (1974)|creature construction]] approach:

> The internal cognitive structure of an [[Agent|agent]] is a *rational* consequence of the tasks it must solve and the resource constraints it faces.

$\quad$ Icard suggests a program for using resource-rationality as a framework for identifying what cognitive traits—including *attitudes* like belief and *capacities* like metacognition—are “inevitable”. #concept-question  inevitable in what sense?

---

# Highlights

[[@2025icardResource]]

- **Psychological concepts are theoretical constructs, defined by their role in models of bounded optimization:** “The aim here will be to explore the possibility that many cognitive structures—including attitudes like beliefs and intentions, as well as capacities for metacognition and metareasoning—might themselves be understood as solutions to problems of resource optimization. In many instances, the relevant adaptation will result from internalizing some key part of the structure we use as theorists to characterize the agent’s predicament.” (126)
- **Resource-rationality as a “species” of Gricean [[Creature construction approach to philosophical psychology, after Grice (1974)|creature construction]]:** “Some version of \[the creature construction\] hypothesis motivates (resource) rational analysis: if we correctly capture the task and sufficiently delineate the resource limitations characterizing the space of solutions, then optimality with respect to that space will be a promising guide to how the system works at some suitable level of abstraction. In other words, there is a kind of inevitability to the structure in question.” (126)
	- #concept-question What precisely is the **creature construction hypothesis**: “If we understand the environmental constraints on a minimally rational agent, we can predict or justify the internal cognitive structures the agent must have.”?

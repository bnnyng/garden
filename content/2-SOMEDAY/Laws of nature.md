# Examples

## In physics

- **Conservation of momentum:** the total momentum of a closed system remains constant.
- [[Newtonian mechanics]]

## In the cognitive sciences

- **Conservation of belief:** implied by the [[Bayesian models of cognition|Bayesian model]] of cognition and [[General definition of probability|axioms of probability]]; increasing the degree of belief in one hypothesis must be accompanied by decreasing the degree of belief in another to maintain a total probability of $1$.

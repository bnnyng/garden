>[!abstract] Theorem: Lagrange
>Let $G$ be a finite [[Algebraic groups|group]]. For each subgroup $H \leq G$, the order of $H$ [[Divisors and prime numbers|divides]] the order of $G$, and we have $|G| = |G/H| |H|$.

- Paradigmatic examples used in philosophy of science, when referencing a mature science

The fundamental law of Newtonian mechanics—that is, the mathematical consequence of Newton’s first two laws, is given by 
$$
\mathbf F = \textup{ma} \quad \quad \text{or} \quad \quad \mathbf F = \textup{m}\frac{d (\textup m \mathbf v)}{dt}.
$$

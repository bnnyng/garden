# Overview

$\quad$ The basic method of Dennett’s intentional attribution—known as the **intentional stance**—is the following ([Shoemaker, 1990](https://www.jstor.org/stable/2026682?seq=1)):

>We take the "intentional stance" toward something when we regard it as being rational, as having the beliefs and desires it "ought" to have, and as being disposed to behave accordingly. Something is an intentional system to the extent that taking the intentional stance toward it is a useful, or essential, way of explaining and predicting its behavior.

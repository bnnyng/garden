$\quad$ The research affiliate position gives me valuable research experience for graduate schools, which I will be applying to in the upcoming year. In particular, my institution, Columbia University, does not have labs using the computational cognitive science methods that I am most interested in; working in Dr. Ho’s lab at NYU gives me the opportunity to learn these practical techniques. Further, participating in lab meetings and journal clubs gives me a better grasp of the current cognitive science literature, which, importantly, helps me identify potential advisors and departments to apply to as I refine my interests. 

$\quad$ For this summer, I will be working—with Dr. Ho’s mentorship—on an independent research project on group problem-solving, using methods from multiagent reinforcement learning. I plan to extend this project to my year-long senior thesis in Cognitive Science in the 2025-2026 academic year.

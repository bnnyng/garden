$\quad$[[@2022levinTechnological]] argues that an agent’s cognitive sophistication is determined by **stress**, the systemic response to a difference between between an agent’s current state and a desired set point: “The scope of states that an agent can possibly be stressed by, in effect, defines their degree of cognitive capacity.”

$\quad$Stress helps an agential network “scale up its agency” by creating bidirectional, **positive** (i.e., change-promoting) **feedback loops**: a sub-agent that seeks to change local conditions will “propagate its happiness” using a signal that mimics the stress machinery of other sub-agents, which react to reduce their own perception of stress.

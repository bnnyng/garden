**Formal logic** studies [[Deductively valid inferences are necessarily truth-preserving as a matter of form alone|deductive validity]], while **metalogic** is concerned with validity of inferences (most valid inferences do not concern validity!). Western formal logic was invented by Aristotle. 

As an example of a *metalogical* claim, Aristotle argued that there are 14 valid forms of inference, each determined by the quantifiers attached to the sentences and the positions of the terms.

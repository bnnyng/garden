# Highlights

[[@2009gallagherMental]]

 - For Hegel, social institutions, like cultural practices and legal systems, are pieces of the mind, externalized in their specific time and place. We create these institutions via our own (shared) mental processes. We then use these institutions instrumentally to do further cognitive work—i.e., to solve problems and to control behavior. (49)
 - Such institutions \[of civil society] are the result of human cognitive processes (they are externalizations of individual minds working collectively) but they are also employed in a cognitive manner to solve problems and to control behavior. (50)

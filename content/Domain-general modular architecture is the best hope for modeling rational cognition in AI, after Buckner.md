[[@2024bucknerDeep|Buckner (2024)]] advocates for [[§ Deep Learning]] research inspired by **moderate empiricism**, according to which **[[Domain specificity and domain generality are graded phenomena, after Laurence and Margolis|domain-general]] modular architecture (DoGMA)** is the most promising method of modeling rational cognition in artificial systems. In contrast to the purely philosophical [[Nativist and empiricist positions in cognitive science|nativism-empiricism]] debates of the past, Buckner emphasizes that DoGMA is a practical theory:

>The name should constantly remind empiricists that the DoGMA must be viewed as an *empirical hypothesis* which should be retained only as long as it remains consistent with the balance of empirical evidence and continues to support a fruitful research program.

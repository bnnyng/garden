#### [[PHIL-UN3551|Philosophy of Science]]

- Last time: contrasting governance picture with descriptivist/Humean picture
	- Exceptionlessly true – deterministic
	- Anarchic, not accomodating to scientific enterprise
- Why does the world have the particular patterns it does?
	- Humean: it’s just there. It’s the bedrock of the metaphysical structure
- Nietzsche’s fear: explanatorily inert, useless
	- Humean says “I’m completely upfront here”
- Explanation tells you how the event in question fits into the general patterns of events that you are used to
	- When someone asks “why did thing turn out this way,” they are not seeing how the event fits into the T-shirt patterns—anomalous, initial surprise
	- How is this different from the [[Meno's paradox]]…where do you acquire the general patterns?
- Microscopic = language of the mosaic
	- Once you get there, Humean says, “My spade is turned”…there’s nothing else to say. … Laughable and empty verbiage in the last step
	- Necessitarian? Property heaven…a final triumph
- Epistemological difference between humean and necessetarian
	- Supervenience = 
	- “Facts about A supervene on B if you can read off all the facts about B”
		- Domain of mental supervenes on domain of the physical: could you read off people’s mental states based on the physical
		- Chemical facts supervene on fundamental physical facts if there could not be two possible worlds where physical facts are the ssame but different chemical facts; physical facts completely determine chemical
- Descriptivist thinks that facts about what the laws are supervene on the mosaic
	- NEcessetarian not true
		- Example: world with one particle. No forces, and the world could have any number of laws compatible with this mosaic. No mechanical procedure for reading from the procedure.
			- Laws fail to supervene on the mosaic—no grounds for eliminating other hypotheses (related to [[The method of hypothesis and problems of indiscriminate confirmation]])?
			- For Humeans, just look for most precise propositions—straightforward reading.
- Humeans think laws supervene on particulars
	- Clear account of how we can discover laws in the first place
- Note that they are both realists about the world, just different metaphysical structure
- “What are the chances that the mosaic has all these patterns?”
	- Probabilities are part of what the laws are…summaries of the mosaic. This assumes that we could exist in a mental space where we even ask that question.
	- #concept-question  Still don’t really get what a probability is in this case.

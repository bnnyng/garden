#### [[MATH-GU4042|Modern Algebra II]]

- [[Polynomial rings]]
	- Ring of constant polynomials
	- Polynomials as a *formal object*; define functions not the same as the function being defined?
- Formal power series
- Can’t do infinite sums in algebra – need convergence
	- Upshot: can’t evaluate them (vs. polynomial)
	- Algebraic objects are different from analytical ones?
- $(\mathbb Z [\sqrt 2 ])^*$ is infinite.
	- Element $( 1 + \sqrt 2)$ has infinite roder, since $|1 + \sqrt 2 | \geq 1$ and hence $( 1 + \sqrt 2) ^n \neq 1$ for all $n \in \mathbb N$.
	- $\langle ( 1 + \sqrt 2 ) \rangle \leq (\mathbb Z [\sqrt 2 ])^*$
	- Point: groups of units can be interesting even if the original ring isn’t that weird

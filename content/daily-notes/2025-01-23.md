#### Many Minds

- Rationalizing is a way of learning – e.g., someone tells you not to do something, and then you rationalize why that is the case.
```
one way to think about how learning by
thinking occurs is to basically say that your mind has multiple
components. One part of your mind knew something, but the other part
of your mind didn’t know that. The way that learning by thinking
works is that Part One tells Part Two. And now Part Two learned
something that it didn’t know before. Basically, if you think about it
like this, you’re reducing the problem of how learning by thinking
works to how it is that we learned from other people, which I think is
much less mysterious.
```

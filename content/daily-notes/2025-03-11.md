#### [[2025-codec-lab|CoDec Lab]] meeting: Marcell Vazquez-Chanlatte

- “RAD” embeddings: https://rad-embeddings.github.io
	- Original paper: https://openreview.net/forum?id=6KDZHgrDhG
- Motivation: general-purpose robot/agent, has to perform a specific task at run time (**task-conditioned RL**)
	- Important: agent doesn’t know what task to perform a prori (i.e., have to do some planning at run time, vs. traditional RL where objective is fixed ahead of time)
- Main idea: Tasks are represented as **deterministic finite automata**. Why?
	- Unambiguous, as opposed to natural language (though can interpolate between them?)
	- Supports temporal behaviors/sequencing, as opposed to goals; “simplest structure that encodes temporal sequences”
	- Explicitly captures additional memory to plan; given a non-Markovian task, tells you explicitly what to manipulate in the state space?
	- Easy for non-experts to interpret
- Implementation: Main encoding is a Graph Neural Net
- Why not just hierarchical?
	- RAD enables **emergent** multi-step planning

![[Pasted image 20250311151727.png]]

- Pipeline with training
	- Encode, pass to CNN and concatenate with observation, then goes to policy network and distribution over actions
	- Training over PPO (actor-critic?)
- The DFA is encoded separately, network doesn’t need to ever see the DFA: “**frozen embeddings**”

![[Pasted image 20250311151831.png]]

- Pretraining DFA encoder: DFAs are themselves [[Markov decision processes and dynamic programming|Markov decision processes]]
- Encoder is a message passing neural network (GATv2): https://paperswithcode.com/method/mpnn
	- Input: information is sent backwards along edges, every token is one state?
	- “Accumulation algorithm” that turns neighbor’s messages into new representation for self.

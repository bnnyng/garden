#### [[NSBV-BC3386|The Neural Code]] debate

- Slides
	- One with quote on epistemology, reframing the question: “Is it possible to reduce the spiking interactions of neurons to the interaction of rates?”; the rate-based perspective is a *method* and not a fact of the world
	- Summary slide with concrete evidence (e.g., [[@1995mainen]])
- Potential rebuttals
	- Variability/lack of reproducibility
		- Individual neurons are deterministic; individual neurons are *not* firing randomly; intrinsic noise is very low [[@1995mainen]].
		- Noise probably reflects something important in the context
	- Difference is about timescales
		- Neurons do not see timescales. They see spike trains
		- If rate is the basis of information processing, then the neuron must produce its own rate $r$ from the presynaptic rates $r’$, which is a number it only accesses via spike trains. How can it compute this in a way that doesn’t differ across trials?

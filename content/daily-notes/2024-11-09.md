- *(Theorem 9.1)* **Taylor's theorem.** Given a function $f$ which is $C^{n+1}$, meaning $f$ is $n + 1$ times [[Multivariable derivatives|differentiable]] and the associated derivative $f^{(n+1)}$ is [[Continuity|continuous]]:
	- (a) $f(x+h) - P_n(h) = \int^h_0 \frac{f^{(n+1)}(x+t)}{n!} (h-t)^n dt$
	- (b) If $f$ is $C^{(n+1)}$ on $[x, x+h]$ in particular and $M = \text{max}_{[0,h]} |f^{(n+1)}(x+t)|$, then $$ |f(x+h) - P_n(h)| \leq \frac{M}{(n+1)!}h^{n+1} $$
	- (c) The error term for $|f(x+h) - P_n(h)|$ is $o(h^n)$. That is, the error decays as fast as some multiple of $h^{n+1}$, and in particular faster than any multiple of $h^n$.

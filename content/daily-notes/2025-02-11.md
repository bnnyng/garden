>[!abstract] Proposition (Hatcher 1.33): Lifting criterion for the existence of general lifts
>Let $W$ be [[(Path-)connectedness|path-connected]] and locally path-connected, $f (W, w_0) \to (X, x_0)$ be continuous, and $p : (Y, y_0) \to (X, x_0)$ be a [[Covering maps|covering map]]. Further, suppose the [[Induced homomorphism between fundamental groups|induced homomorphisms]] satisfy $\textup{Im}(f_*) \subseteq \textup{Im}(p_*)$, meaning $$  f_*([\gamma]) = [f \circ \gamma] = [p \circ \tilde f \circ \gamma] = p_*[\tilde f \circ \gamma]. $$ Then there exists a *unique* $\tilde f : W \to Y$ such that $f = p \circ \tilde f$ and $\tilde f(w_0) = y_0$.

*Sketch of proof from [[MATH-GU4053|Algebraic Topology]].* 

The general strategy is to combine path-connectedness of $W$ and [[Lifting properties of covering spaces|path lifting]] for $p : (Y, y_0) \to (X, x_0)$ to come up with a candidate for $\tilde f$:
1. Choose a path $\lambda : I \to W$ such that $\lambda(0) = w_0$, and suppose we have some $\lambda(1) = w$. Let $\gamma = f \circ \lambda$ so that $\gamma (0) = x_0$. 
2. Apply the [[Lifting properties of covering spaces|path lifting]] theorem to get a unique $\tilde \gamma$ such that $\gamma = p \circ \tilde \gamma$. Then using the base points specified for $p$, we have $$ \tilde \gamma (0) = y_0, \quad \quad f(w) = \gamma(1) = p (\tilde \gamma(1)). $$
3. Set $\tilde f(w) = \tilde \gamma (1)$.

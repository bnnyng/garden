##### The possible deductive argument


- C1: Pragmatics of language are epiphenomena of reasoning.
	- ~Humans and animals both have transient culture (e.g., playing games, yet humans are the only ones who reason.
	- Humans have more complex social relationships that other animals.
	- SC1: Hence, human reasoning is a technology that helps us maintain complex social relationships and coordinate.
	- In simpler social relationships, one can depend on baseline trust in virtue of the strength of that relationship.
	- Complex human relationships mean that baseline trust is insufficient (for…?)
	- SC2: Hence, a function of reasoning is to convince others and share knowledge in the absence of baseline trust; see [[@2017mercier]].
	- SC1 $\to$ Reasoning allows people to indicate the motivation and justification for ideas and actions; let others know about mutual expectations.
	- Pragmatics is just mutual expectations.
	- SC3: Hence, pragmatics in language is an epiphenomena of language being used for reasoning with other people.
- C2: Reasoning is a game that has rules.
	- SC1: (Deductive) reasoning is dialogical, with at least two players; see arguments from [[@2020dutilhnovaes]].
	- SC2: The goal of the reasoning game is to have best ideas win, and people have common interest in solving problems [[@2017mercier]].
- C3: Culture is the reasoning game with a record of multiple rounds.
- C4: Large language models fail at pragmatics not because they do not have the causal world model, but because they have no agency to play the game.
	- Games require agency; see [[@2015zimmerman]], citations in [[@2024dedeo]].
	- LLMs cannot use language like people do; cultural immersion is inadequate.
	- Need something with experience playing the game; to teach via life experience—[[Life experience is incompressible]].
- Problems: underlying conclusion becomes stupidly simple!
	- LLMs cannot have pragmatics because they don’t have agency.
	- My contribution: agency is inadequate, since reasoning is not an enhancement of individual cognition. Rather, needs to already have a social climate that requires something beyond mere trust.
	- Do animals “reason”?
	- Same argument as “LLMs do not have intrinsic motivation or agential autonomy”; my contribution is that human intrinsic motivation and autonomy comes from reasoning, which comes from social trust, and hence LLMs cannot use language with the pragmatics we have unless there’s a similar sort of motivation.
- We cannot build an intelligence from the artifacts of our social relationships.
	- What kind of intelligence is an LLM?
	- LLMs give a new form of cognition: one that has all the resultant features of human relationships but has not done the work of actually establishing those relationships.
	- If LLMs were pitted together, what might happen? Languages and games.

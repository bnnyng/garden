#### [[MATH-GU4053|Algebraic Topology]]

>[!abstract] Theorem: van Kampen
>If $X = \bigcup_\alpha U_\alpha$ is an open cover such that the intersection $U_\alpha \cap U_\beta$ is [[(Path-)connectedness|path-connected]] for all $\alpha, \beta$ and there is some $x_0 \in U_\alpha$ for all $\alpha$, then $\pi_1(X, x_0)$ is generated by $$ \bigcup_\alpha \textup{Im}((i_\alpha)_* : \pi_1(U_\alpha , x_0) \to \pi_1(X, x_0)), $$ the union of the images of all homomorphisms [[Induced homomorphism between fundamental groups|induced]] by inclusion mappings $i_\alpha: U_\alpha \to X$.

(Prove using [[(Lemma) Lebesgue numbers exist for compact metric spaces|Lebesgue number]])

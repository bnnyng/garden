$\quad$The top-down and bottom-up approaches are two different *epistemological* strategies for modeling cognition across different [[Levels of cognitive theories]]:

| Name      | Starting point                                                                            | Relevant data                                                   | Examples                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Top-down  | Theoretical frameworks and assumptions about the structure or goals of a cognitive system | Task structure and environment statistics; behavioral responses | **Normative:** Bayesian models, (resource-)rational analysis<br><br>**Descriptive:** theory-theory of concepts, symbolic rule-based models (e.g., ACT-R) |
| Bottom-up | Observed behaviors and neural processes                                                   | Physiological                                                   | Connectionist models, embodied cognition                                                                                                                 |

>[!question] #concept-question 
>- Where does something like [[§ Reinforcement Learning|reinforcement learning]] (normative, yet data-driven) fall?

>[!question] #open-question 
>- How do *metaphysics*—particularly the multiple realizability of brain functions—change the epistemology (see: [[@2022caoMultiple]])?

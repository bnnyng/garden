There are two senses in which **intuition** is used in the context of mathematics:
- **Psychological:** a mental state that reflects the mathematicians’ knowledge of the rules of practice (i.e., having an intuition for how to solve a problem);
- **Epistemological:** an impression or conviction about the *literal* truth of a (non-logical) mathematical statement.

- Wip: [[@1907russellRegressive|"The Regressive Method of Discovering the Premises of Mathematics" (Russell, 1907)]], intuition-based epistemology, [[The epistemological priority of mathematical principles typically precedes their logical priority, after Russell (1907)]]

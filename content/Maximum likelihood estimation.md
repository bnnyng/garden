$\quad$ **Maximum likelihood estimation** is a way of obtaining a **point estimate** of the parameters of a statistical model that maximize the likelihood of some observed data.

>[!definition] Equation: Maximum likelihood estimation
>$$
>\hat \theta _{MLE} = \arg \max_\theta P (D, \theta)
>$$

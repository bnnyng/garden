The **continuum hypothesis** for infinite sets has the following equivalent characterizations:
- There is no set with cardinality strictly between that of the integers and the real numbers;
- Any subset of the real numbers is either finite, countably infinite, or has the cardinality of the real numbers;
- There is a bijection between any connected interval of the reals and the whole thing;
- The reals are the next largest set after the natural numbers.

It is a consequence of Gödel’s [[Undecidability and incompleteness theorems|incompleteness]] results that the [[Standard axioms of set theory]]—Zermelo–Frankel set theory and the Axiom of Choice—cannot solve CH.

In contrast to deterministic laws of [[Newtonian mechanics]], where initial state of the world fixes its trajectory through physical state spaces, **biological evolution** is **path-dependent**: given the same initial state, the process of evolution “traces out an enormous array of alternative trajectories” for biological systems ([[@2015walker]]).

# Assignment

Five pages, double spaced. 

```
1) Discuss the positivist conception of what scientific theories are about. Talk about the
different varieties of positivism, and about the motivations for positivism, and about
two of the problems with the positivist program.
2) Compare the positivist and the structuralist conceptions of scientific theories.
3) Compare the positivist and the constructive empiricist responses to the problem of the
underdetermination of theory by experiment.
4) What is the instrumentalist conception of scientific theories? Compare instrumentalism
with positivism. How does instrumentalism propose to overcome the objections to
positivism?
5) How does scientific realism propose to respond to the problem of the
underdetermination of theory by experiment? Compare with constructive empiricism.
6) Describe, and discuss, two ways of resisting the claim that all of science reduces to
physics.
7) Make up a topic of your own, and run it by me.
```

>[!abstract] Theorem: Tube lemma
>If $Y$ is compact and $N \subseteq X \times Y$ is an open set containing $\{ x_0\} \times Y$ for some $x_0 \in X$, then there exists a neighborhood $W \subseteq X$ of $x_0$ that 
>$$
>\{ x_0 \} \times Y \subseteq W \times Y \subseteq N.
>$$

^THM-tube-lemma

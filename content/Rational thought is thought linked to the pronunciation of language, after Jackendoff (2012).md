According to [[@2012jackendoff]], the [[Dual process theory of reasoning|dual processes]] of reasoning are distinguished by “System 1” thinking being linked to the pronunciation of language:

>Since pronunciation is linear and discrete, rational thought is linear and discrete. Since pronunciation is slow compared to the speed of thought itself, rational thought is slow. Since thought is unconscious, we can gain conscious access to it only if it has a conscious “handle” such as pronunciation. Since only humans have language, only humans have System 2.

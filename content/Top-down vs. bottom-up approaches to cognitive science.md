There are two different *epistemological* approaches to studying the [[Classical levels of cognitive theory]] or [[Levels of granularity in computational systems]], particularly human cognitive processes: the **top-down** approach begins with behavioral data (see: [[@1982marrVision]], [[@2017jonas]]), while the **bottom-up** approach begins with physiological (e.g., neuroscience) data.

While the epistemological issue relates to which is a more *effective* research strategy, [[@2022caoMultiple]] points out that how [[Multiple realizability and substrate independence|multiply realized]] brain functions are—that is, where to draw the line between “hardware” and “software” in a [[Levels of granularity in computational systems|computational system]]—is a *metaphysical* problem.

#open-question  *How do metaphysics—the multiple realizability of brain functions—change the epistemology?*

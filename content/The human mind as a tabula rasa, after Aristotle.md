In *De Anima* (III, 429b–430a), Aristotle uses the ***tabula rasa***—a wax-covered tablet which Greek academies used for notes, which were smoothed for reuse by heating them until the wax melted—as a metaphor for the infant’s mind.

>[!quote] Aquinas, *De Veritate 2.3.19*. “The Peripatetic Maxim.”
>*Nihil est in intellectu quod non sit prius in sensu* (Nothing in the mind which is not first in the senses).

This metaphor is commonly invoked by empiricist philosophers such as John Locke and David Hume.

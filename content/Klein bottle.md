# Overview and basic construction

>[!example] Definition: Klein bottle
>The **Klein bottle** is the [[Quotient spaces|quotient space]] $[0, 1] \times [0,1] / \sim$, or the unit square modulo the relations $$ (0, t) \sim (1, t) \quad \quad (t, 0) \sim (1-t, 1). $$

# In algebraic topology

- [[Group actions on topological spaces]]: The Klein bottle is homeomorphic to a group of transformations acting on $\mathbb R$. 
- [[(Theorem) A group acting on a simply connected space is isomorphic to the fundamental group of its orbits|Calculating fundamental groups]]: By the previous homeomorphism, the Klein bottle gives an example of a upshot a [[Fundamental groups|fundamental group]] group that is *not* [[Abelian groups|abelian]].

$\quad$ [[@2024goldstoneEmergence]] gives a framework for characterizing group-level role specializations in terms of five core features:

>[!definition] CARMI framework for the emergence of specialized roles
>Members of a group:
>- Communicate their plans and proposals for coordination;
>- Adapt to feedback on how well they are doing at a task;
>- Are repulsed by their partners’ strategies;
>- Develop multi-level plans to facilitate the division of labor;
>- Create internal models of their partners’ intentions and knowledge.

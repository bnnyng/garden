# Overview

In cognitive science, **mind-reading** or **theory-of-mind (ToM)** refers to the ability to make inferences about others’ hidden mental states (e.g., goals, beliefs, emotions).

Theory-of-mind includes the ability to understand intentions from actions ([[@2021wuToo]]). This can be modeled computationally using **inverse planning**, which is a paradigm for inferring mental states what assumes agents are [[Rationality in cognitive science|rational]] actors.

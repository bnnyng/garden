# Decision subproblems


>[!definition] Decision subproblem
>Let $\mathcal S, \mathcal A$ be sets of possible states and actions, respectively, and suppose $\sim$ is an [[Relation|equivalence relation]] on $\mathcal S$ and on $\mathcal A$. A **decision subproblem** induced by $\sigma$ is a tuple $\mathcal D = \langle \mathcal S^\sim, \mathcal A ^\sim, u ^\sim \rangle$, where:
>- $\mathcal S^\sim, \mathcal A ^\sim$ are the $\sim$-classes of $\mathcal S, \mathcal A$, respectively;
>- The map $u : \mathcal S^\sim \times \mathcal A ^\sim \to \mathbb R$ assigns a **utility** $u^\sim([s], [a])$ for pairs of [[Abstraction and idealization|abstractions]] $[s] \in \mathcal S^\sim, \ [a] \in \mathcal A^\sim$.

According to [[@1966hempel]], there are two basic requirements for an explanation to be scientific:
- **Explanatory relevance:** “The explanatory information adduced offers good grounds for believing that the phenomenon to be explained did, or does, indeed occur” (note that exactly what kind of connection Hempel has in mind is unclear).
- **Testability:** “Statements constituting a scientific explanation must be capable of empirical test.”

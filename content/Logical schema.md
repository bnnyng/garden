A **schema** is an *infinite array* of axioms. This is generally the case for axioms in **first-order logic**; for example, the axiom of [[(Proof pattern) Mathematical induction|induction]] is actually a infinite number of axioms, one for each **condition** $\Phi$ expressible in the formal language.

$\quad$ The **”sandwich model” of mind** is a picture of the relation between an agent and the world:
- The world, or “environment,” produces “data” for the agent in terms of perceptual input, which transforms their internal, or “mental,” state;
- The agent chooses actions on the basis of their current mental state, potentially transforming the environment.

$\quad$ This view appears in many literatures, including [[§ Reinforcement Learning]] (e.g., [[Markov decision processes]]).

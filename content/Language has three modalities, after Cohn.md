According to (cognitive?) linguist [Neil Cohn](https://disi.org/a-new-picture-of-language/), humans have an innate ability to express language in three modalities: vocalization, gesturing, and graphics-making. Cohn emphasizes that these are not different options for a general “amodal capacity” for language, but parallel systems. 

- Changes the ontology – language is not assumed to be one thing.

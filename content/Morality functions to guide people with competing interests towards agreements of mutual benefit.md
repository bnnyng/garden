$\quad$ Humans face problems of **interdependent choice**: what choices should we make, given that our actions are intertwined with and affect the actions of others? Across empirical studies of human morality (e.g., psychology, anthropology, biology, economics), there is widespread consensus that the *function* of morality is to find solutions to these problems that achieve **mutual benefit**, meaning both parties are better off than they would be otherwise.

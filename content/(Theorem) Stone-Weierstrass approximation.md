>[!abstract] Theorem: Stone-Weierstrass approximation
>Let $f:[a,b] \to \mathbb R$ be a [[Continuous functions|continuous]] function. For all $\epsilon > 0$, there exists a polynomial $P:[a,b] \to \mathbb R$ such that 
>$$
>\sup_{x \in [a,b]} |f(x) - P(x)| < \epsilon.
>$$

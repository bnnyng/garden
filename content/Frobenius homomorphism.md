# Basic definition

>[!example] Definition: Frobenius homomorphism
>Let $R$ be a [[Algebraic rings|ring]] with prime characteristic $p$, meaning that there is some prime integer $p > 0$ such that for all $r \in R$, we have $p \cdot r = 0$. Then the **Frobenius [[Ring homomorphisms and isomorphisms|homomorphism]]** $\sigma_p : R \to R$ is defined by $$ \sigma_p(r) = r^p $$ for all $r \in R$.

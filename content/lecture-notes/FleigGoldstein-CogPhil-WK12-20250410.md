# Week 12, Day 2: Title
---

- **Cultural evolution** is a parallel process to Darwinian evolution: “**Differential reproduction** is all you need for evolutionary processes.”
- **Niche construction** involves a feedback process where organism and environment mutually influence each other.
	- [[Humans are the niche constructors par excellence, after Kydd]]
	- *Example.* $\quad$ Farming selects for different attributes in humans than hunting-gathering, and likely highly changed selection processes for humans. Common example is lactose tolerance gene into adulthood.
- ***Cognitive* niche construction** is based on the idea that certain cognitive strategies are more attuned to specific environments, and we construct our environments to be more conducive to those strategies.
- *Question.* $\quad$ To what extent does cultural evolution influence genetic evolution? Does the distribution of memes in a population affect the distribution of genes?

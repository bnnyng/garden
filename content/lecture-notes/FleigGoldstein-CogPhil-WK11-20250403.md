# Week 11, Day 2: Attention and internal monologues
---

**On writing philosophical papers**

- Content should involve narrowing on an issue and developing an argument for it (e.g., a frustration from class).
- Treat writing as merely a way of explicating ideas, which will inevitably be refined later.

**Discussion: AI and internal monologues**

- *Discussion.* $\ \text{}$ Why is there opposition to the idea of AI having an internal monologue?
- *Discussion.* $\ \text{}$ Do we internally perform statements before we say them? Is this just a form of rehearsal?
- *Discussion.* $\ \text{}$ Does having an internal monologue make the output better?
- The **bicameral mind** theory is that the internal monologue and (self-)consciousness emerged between the Iliad and the Odyssey. The idea is that the experience of a voice in your head in the Iliad becomes attributed to the self by the time of the Odyssey.

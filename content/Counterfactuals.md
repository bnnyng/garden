**Counterfactual claims** have the following structure: “Had \[false antecedent], \[consequence] would have happened.” For example (see: [[Albert-PhilSci-WK3-20250205]]):
- “If I hadn’t knocked over the ink, there wouldn’t be a stain on the ground.”
- “If I had dropped by glasses, they would have accelerated toward the earth at $9.8 \ m/s^2$.”

# Overview

$\quad$Taking inspiration from Hegel’s work on “objective spirit,” [[@2009gallagherMental]] extends [[@1998clarkExtended]]’s concept of the [[4E (embodied, embedded, extended, and enactive) cognition|extended mind]] to social institutions:

> We create these institutions via our own (shared) mental processes. We then use these institutions instrumentally to do further cognitive work—i.e., to solve problems and to control behavior. … Such institutions \[of civil society] are the result of human cognitive processes (they are externalizations of individual minds working collectively) but they are also employed in a cognitive manner to solve problems and to control behavior. (49–50)

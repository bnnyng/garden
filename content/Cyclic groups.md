>[!example] Definition: Cyclic group
>A [[Algebraic groups|group]] $G$ is **cyclic** if there exists an element $a \in G$ such that $$ G = \langle a \rangle = \{ a^k \ : \ k \in \mathbb Z \}. $$ In this case, $a$ is called a **generator** of $G$.

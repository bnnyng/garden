>[!definition] Cyclic group
>A [[Algebraic groups|group]] $G$ is **cyclic** if there exists an element $a \in G$ such that $$ G = \langle a \rangle = \{ a^k \ : \ k \in \mathbb Z \}. $$ In this case, $a$ is called a **generator** of $G$. 

>[!definition] Order of an element
>If $g \in G$ is *any* element, the **order** of $g$ is the smallest positive integer $n$ such that $a^n = e$, where $e$ is the identity element. If no such $n$ exists, the order of $g$ is infinite.

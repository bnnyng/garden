$\quad$**Niche construction** is an evolutionary process where an organism shifts selection pressures by actively changing the environment, which in turn changes gene frequencies in the population; a typical example is beavers building lodges, a behavior that selects for beavers with longer teeth and flatter tails. Another typical example in humans is the **herding niche**, where domestication of cattle for dairy selected for the lactase allele over approximately 300 generations. 

$\quad$In humans, niche construction is closely related to **culture-gene coevolution.**

$\quad$[[@2008kyddSupernatural]] calls the two forms of hominin niche construction that led to the emergence of religious behavior the **fire niche** and the **handaxe niche**, two domains that human excel at over any other animals. The **fire niche** refers to the theory that “cooking effectively externalizes the energetic load required for digestion, \[freeing] more energy for brain metabolism,” while the **handaxe niche** refer to the theory that the use of sophisticated tools selected for genes coding for timing, accuracy, and mental rotation. Religion itself is a form of [[Human technological ratcheting was accelerated by control over altered states of consciousness, after Kydd (2008)|supernatural niche construction]].

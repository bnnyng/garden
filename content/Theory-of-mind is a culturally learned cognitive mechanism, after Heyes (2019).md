$\quad$In contrast to *simulation theory*—emphasizing introspection-based learning—and *theory-theory*—emphasizing testing of self-generated hypotheses against observed behavior—[[@2019heyesCognitive]] argues that [[Theory-of-mind|theory-of-mind]] is a culturally inherited [[Distinctly human cognitive mechanisms arise from selection on cultural variants, after Heyes (2019)|cognitive gadget]]: “Mindreading experts—parents and others—instruct children about the mind, in conversation and by structuring developmental environments” (9).

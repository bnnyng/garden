# Overview and basic definitions

>[!example] Definition: Commutator subgroup, abelianization
>The **commutator subgroup** of any [[Algebraic groups|group]] $G$ is the subgroup of the form $$ G' = \langle a^{-1}b^{-1}ab \rangle. $$ That is, $G’$ is the subgroup of all *finite products* of elements in $G$ of the form $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$. 
>
>The **abelianization** of $G$ is defined as $\textup{ab}(G) = G / G’$, the [[Abelian groups|abelian]] [[Normal subgroups and quotient groups|quotient group]] of $G$ by its commutator subgroup.

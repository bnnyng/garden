>[!theorem] Irreducibles and primitive polynomials in a polynomial ring over a UFD
>Let $R$ be a [[Unique factorization domains|unique factorization domain]] with [[Field of quotients of an integral domain|field of quotients]] $F$.
>- (i) The [[Polynomial rings|polynomial ring]] $R[x]$ is also a UFD.
>- (ii) The [[Irreducible polynomials|irreducibles]] in $R[x]$ are exactly the elements $r \in R$ which are [[Factorization on integral domains|irreducible]]. 
>- (ii) The primitive polynomials are $f \in R[x]$ for which $f \in F[x]$ is irreducible.

*Proof from [[MATH-GU4042|Modern Algebra II]]*.

Related: [[Digital evolution]], [[Three abstract conditions for evolution, after Dennett]]

| Abstract Process       | Biological Evolution                                                                                                                 | Digital Evolution                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Heredity / replication | Replicating genetic molecules (i.e., DNA and RNA)                                                                                    | Copying a data structure (a digital genome) in memory                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Variation / mutation   | Copy errors, genetic recombination                                                                                                   | Randomly perturbing elements in a digital genome                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Selection              | Survival and reproduction as a logical requirement for genetic material to persist; can be artificially implemented (e.g., breeding) | Fitness functions as automated criterions for selection (analogous to artificial biological selection); open-ended competition for limited resources (e.g., artificial nutrients, CPU cycles needed to replicate code, digital storage to write genomes) |

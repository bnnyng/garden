>[!abstract] Theorem: Irreducible elements in the Gaussian integers
>The irreducibles in $\mathbb Z[i]$ are
>- (i) $\pm 1$ and $\pm i$;
>- (ii) Ordinary primes that are equivalent to $3 \mod 4$;
>- (iii) Elements $a + b[i]$, $a, b \in \mathbb Z$, such that $$ N(a + bi) \equiv 1 \mod 4. $$

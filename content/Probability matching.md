$\quad$ In behavioral sciences such as economics and psychology, **probability matching** is the phenomenon where people make choices in proportion to the the true probabilities of those choices, even when those choices don’t reflect the optimal decision strategy. 

---
# Notes


- Boundedly rational

[[@2011eberhardtConfirmation]]

- **Probability matching in a [[Probabilistic reasoning and Bayesian belief updating|Bayesian framework]]:** “Participants select hypothesis $h$ with a probability corresponding to the (posterior) probability of $h$ (assuming constant utilities); in other words, the behavioral response looks like a random sample by the individual participant from her (posterior) probability distribution.”

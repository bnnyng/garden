# The Galois group of a polynomial

$\quad$ In these notes, we assume $F$ is a [[Algebraic fields|field]] of [[Characteristics of rings|characteristic]] $0$. This has the following implications:
- Every finite normal extension of $F$ is a [[Separable, normal, and Galois extensions of fields|Galois extension]];
- 

>[!definition] Galois group of a polynomial
>Let $F$ be a field of [[Characteristics of rings|characteristic]] zero and $f \in F[x]$ be a *nonconstant* polynomial (i.e., $\deg f \geq 1$), not necessarily irreducible. The **Galois group** of $f$ over $F$ is the [[Galois groups|Galois group]] $\text{Gal}(E/F)$, where $E$ is a [[Splitting fields|splitting field]] of $f$ over $F$.

**Multi-agent reinforcement learning** is a [[Reinforcement learning|reinforcement learning]] framework where independent agents pursue their own rewards. In standard MARL, agents act independently and have no direct incentive to learn from or teach each other: “decentralized training and decentralized execution, with no shared parameters or state” ([[@2021ndousseEmergent]]). A real-world example of a MARL problem is autonomous driving.

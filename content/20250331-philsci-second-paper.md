# Prompts

1) Describe Hume’s argument against induction – and discuss the Bayesian
response to that argument.
2) Discuss the claim that, if there were no governing, necessatarian sorts of
laws, it is very unlikely that there would be any widespread patterns in the
world.
3) Discuss the response of the Logical Positivists to the problem of the under-
determination of theory by any possible experiment.
4) What is Mach’s worry about the role of time in Newtonian Mechanics, and
how does he respond to that worry?
5) Explain Goodman’s attitude towards the descriptive and the justificatory
problems of induction – and explain how he proposes to solve them.
6) Explain Popper’s attitude towards the descriptive and the justificatory
problems of induction – and explain how he proposes to solve them.
7) Make up a topic of your own – and clear it with me.

According to [[@2020clarke-doaneMorality]], [[Pluralism is the view that any reasonable theory about some entities has a mind-independent truth, after Clarke-Doane]]. Under this view, a mathematical pluralist is [[Mathematical realism|realist]] about mathematical truths who rejects their [[Realism and objectivity are in tension, after Clarke-Doane|objectivity]].

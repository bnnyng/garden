# Overview

$\quad$ In game theory, a **cognitive hierarchy** model is an alternative to equilibrium theory that explains empirical behavior in certain games. Within a general cognitive hierarchy model, each player uses iterative (i.e., multi-step) **decision rules** to reason about what other players may be thinking: “Each player believes he understands the game better than the other players” ([[@2004camererCognitive]], 862). 

$\quad$[[@2008yoshidaGame]] characterize a cognitive hierarchy model in “stag hunt” game—which includes both private goals and mutually beneficial goals that require cooperation—as a model of [[Theory-of-mind|theory-of-mind]] with “recursive sophistication”: “My model of your goals includes a model of your model of my goals, and so on ad infinitum” (2).

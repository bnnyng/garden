$\quad$**Autocatalysis** is the process by which a phenomenon maintains itself by **catalyzing** its own production without being consumed. **Autocatalytic networks** or **sets** are groups of chemicals that form a closed loop of catalytic reactions. 

$\quad$Autocatalysis is often considered the basis of the emergence of life in biology (e.g., [[Catalytic closure is the basic property of life, after Kauffman (1995)]]).

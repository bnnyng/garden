$\quad$ In ethical theories, two normative dimensions can be distinguished:

* **Axiology:** roughly, the **good**; descriptions and explanations of the values that “contribute to a person’s welfare, either instrumentally or intrinsically”;
* **Deontics:** roughly, the **right** (i.e., theory of right action); descriptions and explanations of what it means for an “action, policy, or institution to be obligatory, permissible, or impermissible.”

$\quad$This also gives a framework for considering *sources* of values, though not every theory accepts the distinction, nor does the distinction always hold in practical ethical life (see also: [[Human values answer to needs and capabilities, after Alfano (2016)]]).

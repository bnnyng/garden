# Summary

>[!example] Key terms
>


$\quad$ This behavioral economics paper gives a model of reputation formation via teaching. Specifically, “sophisticated players” have an incentive to teach opponents by choosing strategies that have poor payoffs in the short term, but change opponent behavior to benefit the sophisticated player in the long term.

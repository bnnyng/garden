# Key terms

>[!example] Key terms in [[@1966hempel]]
>- **Explanandum phenomenon** = the phenomenon to be accounted for by an explanation; the sentence describing it is the **explanandum sentence**.
>- **Explanans** = the collection of **explanans sentences** that specify the explanatory information.
>- **Covering laws** = the [[Deductively valid inferences are necessarily truth-preserving as a matter of form alone|deductive laws]] invoked in a scientific explanation for some explanandum phenomenon; the explanatory arguemnt is said to **subsume the explanandum** under those laws.

# Background

> ***What are the main theories in the literature? Is there anything missing or problematic?***

- The Bayesian Reciprocator model connects the following important principles for modeling agents from cognitive science, economics, and computer science:
	- **Subjective utility functions:** e.g., as used in [[§ Reinforcement Learning|reinforcement learning]], which express an agent’s general preferences rather than game-specific policies; 
	- **Cooperation in economics:** including the payoffs received by others in one’s own utility function leads to cooperative behavior; valuing others based on one’s beliefs about how similarly they are cooperating leads to **reciprocity**;
	- **Bayesian theory of mind:** inferring utility functions of others helps identify which players to cooperate with.

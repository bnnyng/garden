# Reading notes

- **Four key components of an RL system:**
	- **Policy:** a mapping from perceived states of the environment to actions that can be taken in those states, analogous to **stimulus-response associations** in psychology. A policy completely determines the behavior of a reinforcement learning agent.
	- **Reward signal:** a mapping from state-action pairs to a single number, analogous to immediate experiences like pleasure and pain in biology. In reinforcement learning problems, the goal of the agent is to maximize long-term total reward.
	- **Value function:** the long-term expected reward from starting in a particular state and following a particular policy thereafter. While rewards are immediate and essentially given by the environment, value is an *estimate* based on the agent’s observations.
	- **(Optional) Environment model:** a representation that allows the agents to make inferences about how the environment will behave in situations that involve **planning**, or considering future situations before they are actually experienced.

# Key terms

>[!example] Key terms in [[@1943rosenblueth]]
>- **Output:** any change produced in the surroundings by an object.
>- **Input:** any event external to an object that modifies the object.
>- **Behavior:** any change of an entity with respect to its surroundings.
>	- **Active behavior:** behavior in which the object is the source of output energy in a given reaction; the object may store energy from inputs, but the input is not *directly energizing* the output.
>	- **Passive behavior:** behavior in which all energy in the output can be traced to the immediate input, or the energy remains external to the object throughout the entire reaction.
>		- Examples: throwing and object, soaring flight of a bird.

# Reading notes

- **In contrast to functionalism, *typical* behaviorism does not study the structure and organization of an object:** “…What is meant by the **behavioristic** method of study omits the specific structure and intrinsic organization of the object. … In a **functional** analysis, as opposed to a behavioristic approach, the main goal is the intrinsic organization of the entity studied, its structure and properties; *the relations between the object and the surroundings are relatively incidental*.”

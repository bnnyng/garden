# Key terms

- **Multiple realizability:** “the thesis that the same kinds of mental capacities can be manifested by systems with very different physical constitutions.”
- **Nomological:** a principle, such as a law of nature, that is neither logically necessary nor explainable by other parts of a scientific theory, hence simply taken as true.
- **Neuron doctrine:** the view “that the only functionally relevant aspect of the brain, what gives it its behavioral capacities and gives us our mental states, is neuronal activity, and in particular, patterns of action potentials.”
- **Generative entrenchment:** after Wimsatt (2007); “a deeply generatively entrenched feature of a structure is one that has many other things depending on it because it has played a role in generating them.”

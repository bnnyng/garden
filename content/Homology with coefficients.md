>[!definition] Homology groups with coefficients
>If $X$ is a [[Topological spaces and open sets|topological space]] and $G$ is an [[Abelian groups|abelian]] group, we can define generalized chains $C_p(X; G)$ as the group of formal linear combinations of [[Singular chains and singular homology|singular]] $p$-simplices with coefficients in $G$:
>$$
>C_p(X; G) = G[\text{Sin}_p(X)] = \oplus_{\sigma : \Delta^p \to X} G \sigma.
>$$

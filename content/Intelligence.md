# Definitions of intelligence

- McCarthy (1988, quoted in [[@2023bowlingSettling]]): “Intelligence is the computational part of the ability to achieve goals in the world.”
- [Legg & Hutter (2007)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11023-007-9079-x): Intelligence is the general ability (typically of a single actor) to achieve a diverse set of goals, which is formalized in artificial intelligence research.

#wip 

From [[@2023bowlingSettling]], reward hypothesis and reward-is-enough hypothesis


Silver et al. (2021) propose the related, reward-is-enough hypothesis, which posits that “intelligence, and all of its associated abilities, can be understood as subserving the maximization of reward.”

# Selected passages

- Quine, 1986 (quoted in [[@2022clarke-doaneMathematics]], 15): “I recognize indenumerable infinities only because  they are forced on me by the simplest known systematizations of more welcome  matters. Magnitudes in excess of such demands, e.g., $\omega$ or inaccessible numbers, I look upon only as mathematical recreation and without ontological rights.”

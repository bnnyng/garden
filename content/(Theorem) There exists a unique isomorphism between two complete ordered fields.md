>[!example] Definition: Isomorphism (calculus)
>An **[[Group homomorphisms and isomorphisms|isomorphism]]** between [[Order relations on sets and fields|ordered fields]] $\mathbb F_0, \mathbb F_1$ is a [[Algebra of functions|bijection]] $f : \mathbb F_0 \to \mathbb F_1$ such that:
>-  (a) $f(x + y) = f(x) + f(y)$
>- (b) $f(xy) = f(x)f(y)$
>- (c) $x<y \implies f(x) < f(y)$


> [!abstract] Theorem
> If $\mathbb F_0$ and $\mathbb F_1$ are [[Completeness|complete ordered fields]], then there exists a *unique* [[Group homomorphisms and isomorphisms|isomorphism]] $f: \mathbb F_0 \to \mathbb F_1$.

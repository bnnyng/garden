$\quad$ The **reward hypothesis** is the following claim:

>[!definition] The reward hypothesis
>That all of what we mean by goals and purposes can be well thought of as maximization of the expected value of the cumulative sum of a received scalar signal (reward).

This so-called “expressivity of reward” is a fundamental assumption of [[§ Reinforcement Learning|reinforcement learning]], in which reward must serve as a general-purpose signal that incentivizes an [[Agent|agent]] to take some action.

# Overview

**Transfinite** (now also called **infinite**) **numbers** are used to describe infinitely large sets.

**Related notes:** [[Continuum hypothesis]]

---
# Key examples

- $\omega$**:** the lowest transfinite **ordinal** number, which is also the **order type** of the natural numbers. An example of its use is [[The countably infinite product of the real line with itself]].
- $\aleph_0$**:** the first transfinite **cardinal** number, which is the cardinality of the natural numbers. Assuming the [[Standard axioms of set theory|Axiom of Choice]], the next highest large cardinal is $\aleph_1$; assuming the [[Continuum hypothesis|continuum hypothesis]], $\aleph_1$ is the cardinality of the [[The real numbers|reals]].

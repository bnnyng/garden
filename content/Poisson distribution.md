>[!definition] Poisson distribution
>A [[Discrete random variables and probability mass functions|discrete random variable]] $X \sim \text{Pois}(\lambda)$ has the **Poisson distribution** with parameter $\lambda > 0$ if the PMF of $X$ is 
>$$
>P(X = k) = \frac{e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k}{k!}, \quad \quad k = 0, 1, 2, \ldots.
>$$

>[!definition] Equation: Poisson expectation and variance
>If $X \sim \text{Pois}(\lambda)$, then $$ 
>E(X) = E(X^2) = \lambda.
>$$

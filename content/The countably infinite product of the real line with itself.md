>[!example] Definition: $\mathbb R^\omega$
>The **countably infinite [[Product spaces|product]]** of $\mathbb R$ with itself is given by
>$$
>\mathbb R^\omega = \prod_{i=1}^\infty \mathbb R = \mathbb R ^{Z_{\geq 0}};
>$$ 
>that is, $\mathbb R^\omega$ is the set of all sequences $\{x_n\}_{n \geq 1} = (x_1, x_2, ...)$ in $\mathbb R$.

^90a2bd

$\mathbb R^\omega$ has the following properties:
- Not **[[Local compactness and compactification|locally compact]]** with the [[Product spaces|product topology]], since no basis element lies in a compact subset

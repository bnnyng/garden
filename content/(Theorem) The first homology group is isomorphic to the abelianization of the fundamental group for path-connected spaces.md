>[!theorem] [[MATH-GU4053|Algebraic Topology]] notes 6.1.5, Hatcher 2A.1: The first homology group is the abelianization of the fundamental group for path-connected spaces
>If $X$ is [[(Path-)connectedness|path-connected]], the [[Ring homomorphisms and isomorphisms|homomorphism]] $\textup{ab}(\pi_1(X, x)) \to H_1(X)$, the map induced by $\varphi$ sending the [[Commutator subgroups and abelianization|abelianization]] of the [[Fundamental groups|fundamental group]] to the first [[Singular chains and singular homology|singular homology group]], is an [[Group homomorphisms and isomorphisms|isomorphism]].

*Proof from [[MATH-GU4053|Algebraic Topology]]* ([[2025-02-25]]).

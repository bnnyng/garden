Psillos (1999) describes three claims that together constitute **scientific realism**:
- **Metaphysical stance:** the world has a definite, mind-independent natural-kind structure.
- **Semantic stance:** scientific theories should be taken as face value and can be taken as true or false.
- **Epistemic stance:** “mature and predictively successful scientific theories” are approximately true of the world, i.e., approximately describe entities that do inhabit the world.

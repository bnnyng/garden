# Overview

$\quad$ Iterated reasoning, cognitive hierarchy, and recursive theory-of-mind are related models of [[(Resource-)rational analysis|boundedly rational]] strategic thinking in [[§ Game Theory|games]], but differ in their disciplinary origins and modeling goals. All three approaches have a “level-0” *anchor* or starting point of reasoning that does not respond to other players’ strategies, and which higher levels of reasoning build upon.

|                                | Iterated (level-$k$) reasoning                                                                                                         | Cognitive hierarchy                                                                                                                         | Recursive TOM                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Disciplinary origin**        | Behavioral economics; developed to explain deviations from [[Equilibria in games of strategy\|Nash equilibrium]] in human experiments. | Behavioral economics; refinement of level-k to better fit experimental data.<br><br>Source(s): [[@2004camererCognitive]].                   | Computational neuroscience and cognitive science; developed to connect recursive reasoning models with *mechanisms* of social cognition.                                                |
| **Modeling target**            | Fit model aggregate, group-level behavior; each subject in an experiment is assumed to belong to a discrete level type.                | Fit model aggregate, group-level behavior; instead of fixed arbitrary proportions, the distribution of levels in the population is Poisson. | A [[Rational process models of cognition\|rational process model]] of how an cognitive agent implements iterated reasoning and dynamically updates beliefs about their partner’s level. |
| **Individual player strategy** | A level-$k$ player takes the best response to all other players being level $k-1$.                                                     | A level-$k$ player takes the best response to the other players being a *distribution* of lower levels ($k-1$ and below).                   | An agent takes the best response the *distribution* of possible reasoning depths that they believe their partner has.                                                                   |

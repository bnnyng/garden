$\quad$Literature notes correspond with a single source (book, article, video, etc.). They will generally be formatted with the following sections:

- **Summary:** a paragraph to capture the main ideas from the text in an informational way, in the same vein as a scientific abstract.
- **Takeaways** or **atomic notes:** ideas and propositional statements that I plan to revisit or apply directly to my life/thinking.
- **Key terms:** context-dependent definitions, often lifted directly from the source material.
- **Reading notes:** paraphrased bullet points and highlights from the source. Additional notation I use includes 💬 for questions and ephemeral observations and 🔎 for empirical results, particularly in otherwise theory-heavy sources.

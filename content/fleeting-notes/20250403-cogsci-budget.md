```
Registration for trainees: $200-$300  
Accommodations: ~$100/night (it's good form to try and find a roommate and split the fee for a room with 2 twin / queen beds!)  
Airfare: ~$300 at the moment (round trip from new york to san diego w/ one carry on) but could get a lot pricier as time goes on  
Meals: depends on the funding source, some allow meals, some don't! I'd say if you do get meals reimbursed, request ~$40-$50/day since people tend to eat out
```

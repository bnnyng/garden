According to linguist [Neil Cohn](https://disi.org/a-new-picture-of-language/), artistic ability is precisely the ability to use a “pattern language” to present the world in an interpretable way. In contrast, most people seem to associate not being able to draw with failing to replicate the kinematics of drawing. 

- Drawing is to graphics-making as gesture is to sign language
- People don’t have an innate ability to draw or not, just a matter of learning the language

#open-question  Are there “natural” organizational principles for graphics-making that are different from vocalizing? What does this say about different languages across modalities—a sort of Sapir-Whorf moment? What about people who know multiple vocal languages, for example, as opposed to one in each mode?

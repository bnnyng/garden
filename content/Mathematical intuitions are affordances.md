# Log

- Sensory organ for the “mathematical world” is reason
	- What is the goal of the senses? To transform information in the environment to a format amenable to more processing
	- Perception is the direct output/form of that perception
	- The most important function of the senses is to detect biologically/motivationally salient *relations*; does not matter if a representation is faithful to the real world in isolation, but only that the (causal?) relationships between things are modeled to a satisficing granularity

According to Dennett [^1], there are three abstract principles underlying biological evolution that are independent of the physical substrate (e.g., DNA or RNA):

> Evolution will occur whenever and wherever three conditions are met: **replication**, **variation** (mutation), and **differential fitness** (competition).

Related: [[Biological vs. digital evolution]]

[^1]: Dennett, Daniel (2002). *Encyclopedia of Evolution.*

>[!theorem] Poisson approximation to binomial
>If $X \sim \text{Bin}(n, p)$ has a [[Bernoulli and binomial distributions|binomial distribution]] and $n \to \infty$ and $p \to 0$ such that $np \to \lambda$ for some constant $\lambda > 0$, then the PMF or $X$ converges to the [[Poisson distribution|Poisson]] PMF
>$$
>\frac{e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k}{k!}.
>$$

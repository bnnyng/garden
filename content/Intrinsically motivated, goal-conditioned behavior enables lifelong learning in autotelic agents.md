$\quad$**Autotelic agents**—from Greek *auto* (self) and *telos* (end, goal)—are [[Agent|agents]] that are both [[Intrinsically motivated behavior is performed for its own sake|intrinsically motivated]] and **goal-conditioned**, meaning they are “intrinsically motivated to represent, generate, pursue, and master their own goals” ([[@2022colasAutotelic]]).

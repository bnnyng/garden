# Quarterly overview

## [[2025-winter|Winter]]

>[!example] Key dates and timeline
>- **January 20** – ~~Internal deadline for [[2025-application-goldwater|Goldwater scholarship]] materials~~
>- **January 21 – 31** – Add/drop period for courses
>- **March 5** – ~~[SRI application](https://barnard.edu/sri/2025-program-details-calendar) due~~

- Start gathering diverse extracurriculars that demonstrate “intellect, character, leadership, and commitment to service” 
	- **Academic:** directed reading program in math (e.g., category theory); teaching assistantship for Analysis I or Discrete Math
	- **Service:** Housing Equity Project
- Maintain 1-2 non-academic hobbies for physical and emotional well-being
	- **Physical:** Pilates, weightlifting, roller skating, pole dancing
	- **Emotional and creative:** CU Artists Society
- Make concrete plans for the summer

## Spring


>[!example] Key dates and timeline
>- **April** – Beyond Barnard [conference funding application](https://barnard.edu/beyond-barnard/funding-student-conference-travel) (budget, essay, resume, letter of support); application is due 30 days before official registration deadlines
>- **May** – [CogSci 2025](https://cognitivesciencesociety.org/cogsci-2025/) early registration


## Summer


>[!example] Key dates and timeline
>- **July 29 – August 2** – CogSci 2025 (San Francisco)

- Prepare for and take the GRE
## Fall

>[!example] Key dates and timeline
>- **October** – Apply to Cambridge prior to Gates-Cambridge deadline, typically second week of October
>- **October 7** – [Fulbright Program](https://us.fulbrightonline.org/applicants/getting-started) deadline

# Overview

[[@2021wuToo]] introduce the **Bayesian delegation** algorithm for modeling “latent intentions” of other agents in order to decide whether to divide-and-conquer subtasks or collaborate on the same subtask. A separate action planner finds approximately optimal policies for each subtask.

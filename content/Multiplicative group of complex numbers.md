# The circle group and roots of unity

>[!example] Definition: Circle group
>The **circle group** is the [[Algebraic groups|subgroup]] of $\mathbb C^*$ defined by $$ \mathbb T = \{ z \in \mathbb C \ : \ |z| = 1 \}. $$

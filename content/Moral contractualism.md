$\quad$ In moral philosophy, **contractualism** is the view that a morally correct action is one that is agreed upon, or at least not reasonably rejected, by all individuals affected. Contractualist judgments or actions can be modeled as solutions to idealized [[Bargaining problems|bargaining problems]], which are situations in which a group of agents have the opportunity to enter a mutually beneficial arrangement.

>[!example] Definition: Series of functions
>Le $(f_n)$ be a sequence of functions defined on a set $f_n : E \to \mathbb R$, and define a sequence $(S_n)$ of [[Series|partial sums]] $S_n : E \to \mathbb R$ by
>$$
>S_n(x) = \sum_{k=1}^n f_k(x)
>$$
>❓ for all x? Then the series
>$$
>S(x) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty f_n(x)
>$$
>converges **pointwise** to $S : E \to \mathbb R$ if $S_n \to S$ **pointwise** (❓ as a [[Convergent sequences of functions|sequence of funcitons]]) as $n \to \infty$, and converges **uniformly** to $S$ on $E$ if $S_n \to S$ uniformly on $E$.

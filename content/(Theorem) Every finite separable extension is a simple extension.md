>[!theorem] Primitive element theorem
>Let $E$ be a finite [[Separable, normal, and Galois extensions of fields|separable extension]] of a [[Algebraic fields|field]] $F$. Then there exists an element $\alpha \in  E$ such that $E = F(\alpha)$. That is, every finite separable extension is a [[Extension fields|simple extension]].

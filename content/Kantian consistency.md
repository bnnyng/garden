[[@2020clarke-doaneMorality]] uses the term **Kantian consistency** to refer to “whether we can will the theory to be universal law without somehow defeating the purpose of acting on it” (p. 39). This is a much stronger property than first-order [[Effectively calculable, recursive, and computable are equivalent descriptions of functions|consistency]].
